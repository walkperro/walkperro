listen — i don't think people realize how life-changing the <mark><em>/goal</em></mark> skill claude code quietly shipped actually is.

if you don't know: typing /goal in your CLI runs tasks on a loop until your goal is complete. it doesn't stop. it just works.

now — be careful. because the first thing the average person (me) is gonna type is "go make me money." and here's the thing — it can't. not like that. claude won't make accounts for you. (i've heard openclaw might — haven't tested it.) so if you walk away thinking you'll come back rich, you'll come back rate-limited with your tokens drained, because the loop has nowhere to send a payment and no way to do outreach.

/goal doesn't fail because it's weak. it fails because you were vague.

you gotta be specific. you have to hand it a goal so detailed it can't spiral.

so i did. i brain-dumped everything i could think of into one sloppy prompt — every constraint, every account i already have, every thing i didn't want. and the output was ridiculous. i promise you — if you need ideas or inspiration on what to build, or what your next financial move is, run this prompt with /goal and you'll walk away with a real sense of direction. you could realistically be making good money within weeks, not months:

> I want you to spend at least 12 consecutive hours doing deep and thorough research, planning, battle-testing, analyzing, and strategizing — and ultimately come up with a feasible business I can run and make money with, with little to no overhead and a short startup time.
>
> I want as little friction as possible. As few new accounts as possible. I already have a Claude Max plan, a Stripe account for payments, Supabase, and the ability to vibe-code websites and apps.
>
> I'd like monthly recurring subscribers — a SaaS, or an item I can easily and massively reproduce. I'd love to make a game-type, cult-like thing. But nothing to do with building websites. I want something that genuinely hits with the masses. I want little to no competition in my field. Check all relevant trends and use only relevant data.
>
> Come up with many different hypothetical directions and analyze each one. Do multiple tests. Use every tool and skill you need. Search and pull the data you need, analyze it, and be a creative, trend-setting genius about it.
>
> I want real, realistic results. Find something with little to no competition but huge opportunity. Analyze the psychology — which age groups to aim for, why people would adopt it, what makes it spread. This is the next big thing. Make it cool, fun, and simple.

the move isn't "tell AI to make you money." the move is to think harder than the AI about what you actually want — then let it bring your intention to life.

now go get it.
