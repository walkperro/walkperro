Here's how it happened.

I just discovered Lucy Guo and was watching some of her interviews. She kept mentioning how she scrapes data from Reddit and other places to find solutions to problems — or to point her and her team toward where to fix a needed issue. So boom. What did I do? I did the same thing.

*"Hey Claude, go scrape data from Reddit and find an opportunity that could be turned into a business at low overhead and low setup time. Claude ran with it. Within minutes of finishing the scrape, it was building me the web app: **automatically linking Stripe for payments, generating my listings, drafting my marketing plan — everything.** All I had to do was connect the Stripe CLI in terminal (or use the Stripe MCP in Claude desktop).
