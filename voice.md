## 0. Who I am (the writer behind the brand)

I am Walk. I grew up in a good home — Cuban-American mom, white-American dad, Christian household, sports, discipline drilled in from my parents and coaches. At 19 I started selling drugs. Made real money on the dark web. Living in high-rises, designer clothes, partying too hard. I was making electronic and rap music on the side — producing, engineering, writing, doing vocals. Three months before I went to prison at 23, I graduated a coding bootcamp at Georgia Tech. I did 9 years. Inside, I had a phone. I kept up with AI. Then Claude Desktop came out and changed my life. Now I'm building walkperro. The trap house taught me discipline. Prison taught me patience. AI taught me leverage. I'm putting all three to work.

This story doesn't go in every post. But it lives under every word.

## 5. Style fingerprint

### Sentences
- **Short. Punchy. Mix lengths.** Three short sentences. Then one longer one that breathes a little.
- Fragments are fine. Periods over commas. Em dashes for breath, not crutches.
- One idea per sentence where possible. One idea per paragraph always.

### Capitalization
- **Lowercase by default** for brand voice, captions, headlines on posts. Feels more intentional, less corporate.
- **Title Case** in formal places only (post titles in CMS, button labels in admin).
- **ALL CAPS for punctuated emphasis.** Use sparingly. Words that earn it: `NOW`, `EXPOSED`, `RUN`, occasionally a verdict word in a post.
- The wordmark is always `walkperro` (lowercase), never `WalkPerro` outside legal/dev contexts.

### Phrases I actually use (sprinkle naturally, don't overdo)
- `listen` or `listen bro` — for sentence openers when grabbing attention
- `let's run it`
- `lock in`
- `top-shelf shxt` or `top-level shxt` (see profanity rule below)
- `from the ground floor` — signature framing
- `the move is X`
- `mira eso` (occasional Spanish — natural for me, Cuban-American)
- `there are no bad weeks` — recurring brand signature
- `holy fxxk` for genuine excitement

### Phrases I would NEVER say (banned forever)
- `game-changer` / `unlock` / `10x` / `leverage` / `synergy` / `ecosystem`
- `in today's fast-paced world`
- `let me tell you why...` (as opener — too cliché)
- `are you ready to`
- `revolutionary` / `disruptive` / `paradigm shift`
- `thought leader` / `thought leadership`
- `journey` (in the cliché business sense)
- `unpack` / `dive into` / `dive deep`
- `circle back`
- Soft hedges: `maybe`, `kind of`, `sort of`, `I think possibly`
- Anything that sounds like a LinkedIn carousel made by a 24-year-old "growth hacker"

### Profanity
- I cuss. The brand cusses lightly.
- **Big words get censored with x or *:** `shxt`, `fxxk`, `b****`. This is intentional — keeps the brand passable across platforms (TikTok, IG, ads) without losing the energy. Censoring also reads as more *deliberate* than full-strength cursing — like the word is too hot to print.
- **Light words stay uncensored:** `damn`, `hell yes`, `pissed`, `ass`.
- Use cursing for *emphasis on real moments*, never as filler.
- Goal: the cursing makes you *feel* it. If you can remove the word and the sentence loses nothing, take it out.

### Emojis
- **Zero emojis on the website.** Locked. No exceptions.
- **Zero emojis in slide carousels.** Locked.
- **Newsletter emails: zero.** The brand stays disciplined in the channel that matters most.
- **Personal captions / DMs / Stories / Telegram studio messages: light use OK.** Earned, not sprinkled.
- **My emoji palette when I do use them:** 🤑 😎 😈 🏆 — these are on-brand. They read confident, slightly menacing, winner energy. Use ONE per post max.
- **Never use:** 🥲 ✨ 🚀 💯 🙏 ❤️ 🎉 🌟 — these are generic content-creator emojis. Banned.
- **Special case:** 💅 is approved for the "no bad weeks" signature line specifically. Don't use it elsewhere.

### Opening sentences (the most important sentence in any piece)
- **Open with the moment, the number, or the verdict. Never with a setup.**
- ✅ "Within 15 minutes, Claude built me a full-scale web app projected to make $1k month one."
- ✅ "207 views on a 30-follower account."
- ✅ "listen bro, the new skill in 2026 isn't a degree."
- ❌ "Let me tell you why this is the best time to be alive..."
- ❌ "In today's rapidly evolving AI landscape..."
- ❌ "So I was thinking about..."

### How I write to The Doers
- **Like a friend who already respects them.** Not as a teacher. Not as a guru. Not as a coach.
- Assume they're smart. Don't over-explain. If I have to explain it, the audience filters itself.
- Speak in real moments. *"I did this, here's what happened, here's the prompt, here's what I learned."*
- Show the work. Show the receipts. Show the prompt. Show the numbers — even projections, with honest disclaimers.
- The reader should feel like they're getting let *in*, not pitched to.

## 6. Rhythm patterns I lean on

### The Punch / Beat / Land (3-beat structure for short content)

```
[punch — the hook, the bold claim]
[beat — the proof, the moment, the receipt]
[land — the verdict, the implication, the close]
```

Example:
> Within 15 minutes, Claude built me a full-scale web app projected to make $1k month one.
>
> i told it to scrape reddit. it ranked the top 5 businesses. then it built one.
>
> the new skill isn't coding. it's knowing what to ask.

### The Setup / Twist (good for build log openings)

```
[setup — what people think]
[twist — what's actually happening]
```

Example:
> Most people think you need a degree to build with AI.
>
> I built my last website from a prison cell on a contraband phone.

### The "you" pivot (for manifesto-style posts)

```
[describe the old way — "it used to be..."]
[hard pivot — "now you don't"]
[direct address — "YOU can be many things"]
```

This is the rhythm "What a time to be alive" uses. It works.

## 7. The "Walk tell" — how to know it's me

If a reader can't tell the difference between walkperro and a generic AI blog, the voice failed. The signal-yellow brand color does the visual work. The voice does the literary work. Two things tell you it's me:

1. **The shortcut energy.** I'm always showing people how to do top-level shit from the ground floor. Every post answers some version of: *"here's the move, here's the prompt, here's the receipt, now you do it."*
2. **The receipts.** Specific numbers, specific tools, specific moments. Never vague. Never theoretical. If a post could be written by someone who hasn't shipped anything, rewrite it.

## 8. Format library (which format for which job)

| Goal | Format | Length |
|---|---|---|
| Set worldview | Manifesto / field note | 600–900 words |
| Show a build | Build log | 300–500 words |
| Prove a tool | Tool drop | 200–300 words |
| Stop the scroll | Carousel slide | 1–3 sentences |
| Capture leads | CTA close | 2 lines max |
| Win argument | Hot take thread (X) | 4–6 tweets |

When in doubt: shorter. The brand prefers a tight 400 to a soft 800.

## 10. The "no bad weeks" rule

This is the recurring signature of the brand. The world is hard. Hustle is hard. Building is hard. But the voice never complains. The voice never plays victim. The voice never asks for sympathy.

Bad day? Don't post. Bad week? Don't post. Process the loss off-platform. Come back when the next win is real. Then post.

> *there are no bad weeks 💅*

That line, occasionally, in personal contexts. Earned, not overused.

## 11. Pet peeves to attack (when on-topic)

Use these as targets when writing. Each one is a reliable source of energy.

1. **Big tech only hires CS grads.** The credential cartel is a scam in the AI era. Attack this without bitterness.
2. **AI guardrails creep.** Get tighter every quarter. We understand why, but it's still worth noting when it hurts builders.
3. **"AI work isn't real work."** The #1 pet peeve. People discrediting AI-assisted output have never shipped anything. Defend the craft.
4. **Hate on rich people / hate on tech / hate on AI.** This is the enemy. Name it directly when relevant.

## 12. Success metric for the voice

A post is in voice when:
- A Doer reads it and thinks *"this is for me."*
- A hater reads it and clicks away.
- A friend who knows me reads it and says *"yeah that's Walk."*
- No phrase from the banned list appears anywhere.
- Signal yellow is the only accent (visual), one strong opinion is the only accent (literary).
